Dataset Provenance & The Temporal Drift Imperative

While the WELFake corpus delivers substantial linguistic diversity across 72,134 balanced records, machine learning models trained on static collections remain vulnerable to temporal language drift. Disinformation strategies constantly evolve to mimic credible, restrained wire prose.

TruthLens strictly enforces that automated outputs function as triage assistance for verified reporters, not as autonomous arbiters of truth:

1. Stylometry Over Facts TF-IDF algorithms identify emotional rhetoric and stylistic anomalies, not empirical ground truths in real-time history.
2. Human-in-the-Loop A score above 85% flags an article for secondary review; no content is automatically suppressed or retracted.
